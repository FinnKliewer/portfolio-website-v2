# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is senior engineering and quant-infrastructure hiring leaders evaluating exceptional technical talent for high-compensation platform, infrastructure, systems, and adjacent engineering roles. Investors, founders, and technically sophisticated collaborators are a secondary audience.

Visitors should quickly understand that Finn is a high-leverage technical operator worth contacting, then be able to inspect evidence through his experience, selected work, résumé, and public code.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio establishes Finn Kliewer as a platform engineer and software systems builder whose work increases the speed, reliability, and leverage of high-performance engineering organizations. Success means the visitor leaves with a clear, credible impression of engineering depth, ambition, judgment, and unusually broad operating range.

## Positioning

Finn combines production software and platform engineering experience with business and markets fluency. The site must present that breadth as compounding technical judgment—not as ambiguity between software engineering, data science, analytics, or business analysis.

## Operating Context

The portfolio is evaluated quickly by engineering leaders, recruiters, founders, investors, and peers on desktop and mobile devices. The visitor may arrive through a résumé, LinkedIn profile, GitHub profile, or direct introduction and needs clear paths to experience, selected work, and contact information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React web portfolio with light and dark themes.
- Routes: Home, Experience, Selected Work, Contact, and a 404 state.
- The Selected Work carousel is a protected implementation boundary. Its components, responsive rules, dimensions, and internal layout must not change during the full-site redesign.
- The site must remain responsive from small mobile screens through ultrawide desktop displays, including intermediate widths around 735px, 830–890px, and 1027–1350px.
- The responsive Vanta mesh effect on the home page is an established feature and must be preserved.
- Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences and cannot compromise content access, navigation, or performance.
- Contact form behavior, résumé access, GitHub data, navigation, theming, and external links must remain functional.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Finn Kliewer.
- Current role: Platform Engineer at Citadel.

## Product Principles

1. Engineering identity is immediately legible; breadth strengthens it rather than diluting it.
2. Demonstrated systems, work, and decisions carry more weight than self-description.
3. The interface should feel precise, consequential, and authored at every viewport.
4. Ambition appears through craft, restraint, and technical specificity—not hype.
5. Every interaction should reinforce the impression of leverage, control, and forward motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic structure, keyboard-operable navigation and controls, visible focus states, sufficient contrast in both themes, responsive reading widths, and a complete reduced-motion experience.
